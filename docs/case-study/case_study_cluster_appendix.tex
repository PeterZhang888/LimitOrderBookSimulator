\subsection{Descriptive targeted-book effects by cluster}
\label{app:r36-target-cluster-diagnostics}

Table~\ref{tab:r36-target-cluster-diagnostics} reports full-session
shock-minus-control spread differences for the targeted books.  These values
are supplementary diagnostics, not cluster-specific causal estimates.  They
average the intervention over the complete 23,400-second session, each cluster
contains only one to three target symbols, and submitted quantities vary with
contemporaneous depth.  The per-second output aggregates all 15 targets and
therefore cannot recover cluster-specific response trajectories after the run.
